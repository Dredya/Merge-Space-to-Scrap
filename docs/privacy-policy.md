# Privacy Policy — Scrap to Space

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

Scrap to Space ("we", "the app") is an offline mobile game. This policy describes how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Information we collect

Scrap to Space is designed to work **offline**. By default:

- We do **not** collect, store, or share any personal information.
- No account or login is required.
- The game does not require an internet connection to play.
- Game progress is stored **locally on your device** and is never transmitted.

## 2. Rewarded ads (optional)

Rewarded ads are optional and only play when you choose to watch them. In the current build the ad provider is a **simulated** one that grants no real ad view.

If real advertisements are enabled in a future update, the relevant ad network (for example Google AdMob) may collect device and usage data as described in that network's privacy policy. When that happens, this policy will be updated and the relevant network's policy will be linked here.

- Google AdMob privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Local data

- Progress data (credits, board, upgrades, ship, expeditions, settings) stays on your device.
- Uninstalling the app, or using **Settings → RESET PROGRESS**, deletes that data.
- We do not back up your data to any server.

## 4. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

## 5. Third-party services

When real ads are enabled, the app may use Google AdMob, which is governed by Google's privacy policy (linked above). No other third-party services are used.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We may update these terms. Continued play after an update means you accept the revised policy.
